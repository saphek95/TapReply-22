Privacy Policy

Your conversations stay yours.

Last updated: September 26, 2026

In short

1

Your screenshot is read on your iPhone and never leaves it.

2

Only the conversation text is sent, with your consent, to an AI that writes your reply. It is never stored and never used to train models.

3

No account, no email, no ads, no tracking, no data sale.

4

You stay in control: turn AI sharing and statistics off anytime in Settings.

1Who we are

TapReply is published by Pierre Madi, France. Contact: contact@saphek.com

3Who receives it

  • Supabase (server hosting, European Union, Paris): relays the request, does not store the text.
  • OpenRouter (United States): routes the request only to AI providers with zero data retention that do not train on your data.
  • Google (Gemini) and Anthropic (Claude) (United States): write the reply in real time.
  • RevenueCat (United States): subscriptions and credits.
  • Apple: payments through the App Store.
  • Vercel (United States): hosts this website (privacy and support pages). The app does not send it any data.

Transfers outside the EU rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

4Your choices and rights

  • Withdraw consent : Settings › AI sharing.
  • Turn off statistics : Settings › Anonymous usage statistics.
  • Erase your history : Settings › Clear all history (stored on your iPhone only).
  • Server data : email us the identifier shown in Settings › Anonymous identifier and we delete it.
  • GDPR rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection and portability; you can contact your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

5Children and changes

TapReply is not intended for children under 13. We will update this page and its date if anything changes; significant changes will be announced in the app.

3Qui la reçoit

  • Supabase (hébergement du serveur, Union européenne, Paris) : relaie la demande, ne stocke pas le texte.

  • Vercel (États-Unis) : héberge ce site (pages confidentialité et assistance). L'app ne lui envoie aucune donnée.

Les transferts hors UE reposent sur les clauses contractuelles types de la Commission européenne ou le Data Privacy Framework UE-États-Unis.

4Tes choix et tes droits

  • Retirer ton accord : Réglages › Partage avec l'IA.
  • Couper les statistiques : Réglages › Statistiques d'usage anonymes.
  • Effacer ton historique : Réglages › Effacer tout l'historique (stocké uniquement sur ton iPhone).
  • Données serveur : écris-nous avec l'identifiant affiché dans Réglages › Identifiant anonyme, et nous les supprimons.
  • Droits d'accès, de rectification, d'effacement, d'opposition et de portabilité (RGPD) ; tu peux saisir la CNIL.
